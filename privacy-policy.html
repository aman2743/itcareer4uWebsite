ITCareer4u.com - Privacy Policy

Welcome to our privacy policy. We have crafted this document to address your concerns regarding the usage of your 'Personally Identifiable Information' (PII) online. PII, as defined by US privacy law and information security standards, comprises data that can individually or in conjunction with other information identify, contact, locate a single person, or ascertain an individual within a specific context. We encourage you to carefully read this policy to gain a clear understanding of how we collect, utilize, protect, and handle your Personally Identifiable Information in compliance with our website.

What Personal Information Do We Collect?

When visiting our blog, website, or app, we may request your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, College, Work Details, or other information to enhance your experience.

When Do We Collect Information?

We collect information when you register on our site, place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, complete a form, or provide information through our site.

How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the information collected for various purposes:

  • Personalize your experience and provide content and product offerings tailored to your interests.
  • Enhance and improve our website for better service.
  • Address customer service inquiries efficiently.
  • Administer contests, promotions, surveys, or other site features.
  • Expedite transaction processing.
  • Request ratings and reviews of our services or products.
  • Follow up after correspondence, including live chat, email, or phone inquiries.

How Do We Protect Your Information?

We do not employ vulnerability scanning or scanning to PCI standards. We solely offer articles and information and do not request credit card numbers. We also do not use Malware Scanning.

Do We Use 'Cookies'?

Yes, we use cookies, which are small files transferred to your computer's hard drive via your web browser (if permitted). Cookies help us remember and process items in your shopping cart, understand your preferences based on past or current site activity, and compile aggregate data about site traffic and interaction to improve future site experiences and tools.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your Personally Identifiable Information to outside parties without advance notice. This excludes website hosting partners and other trusted parties assisting us in website operation and user service, provided they maintain confidentiality. Information may be released when appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect rights, property, or safety. However, non-personally identifiable visitor information may be shared with other parties for marketing, advertising, or other purposes.

Third-Party Links

Occasionally, we may include third-party products or services on our website, which may have separate privacy policies. We take measures to protect our site's integrity but welcome feedback regarding these linked sites.

Google

We utilize Google AdSense Advertising on our website, adhering to Google's Advertising Principles for a positive user experience. Google uses cookies, including the DART cookie, to serve ads based on users' previous visits to our site and other sites on the internet. Users can opt out via the Google Ad Settings page or Network Advertising Initiative Opt Out page.

California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA)

CalOPPA requires commercial websites and online services to post a privacy policy, extending its reach beyond California to any person or company in the United States that collects Personally Identifiable Information from California consumers. We comply with CalOPPA by allowing anonymous site visits and prominently displaying our Privacy Policy link.

Privacy Policy Updates

Any changes to our Privacy Policy will be notified on our Privacy Policy Page.

Do Not Track Signals and Third-Party Behavioral Tracking

We respect Do Not Track signals and do not track, plant cookies, or use advertising when a Do Not Track (DNT) browser mechanism is active. Third-party behavioral tracking may be allowed.

Children Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

We do not target children under 13 years old and do not allow third-parties to collect PII from children under 13.

Fair Information Practices

We follow Fair Information Practices Principles, which underpin privacy law in the United States. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected parties via email and in-site notification within 7 business days.

CAN-SPAM Act

We collect email addresses for purposes such as responding to inquiries, processing orders, sending updates, and marketing. Our emails are not deceptive, include our physical address, and offer an unsubscribe option. To unsubscribe, follow the instructions at the bottom of each email.
